Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Dating Profile Vault is designed with your privacy as a priority. Your dating profile content stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. It is never sent to servers we operate, and we have no way to read it.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. All information you enter into the app—including bios, interests, job titles, locations, and prompt answers—is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The only information that reaches us is the anonymous usage statistics described below, which never include your profile content.

Data Storage

  • All profile data is stored on your device
  • Data persists in your device's local storage
  • App settings are stored locally using UserDefaults
  • No servers of ours, no accounts, no external databases
  • You can require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to open the app (Settings › Privacy)

iCloud Sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your vault in your private iCloud database so it is backed up and appears on your other iPhones. This uses Apple's CloudKit; the data belongs to your Apple ID, and we cannot access it. Your bio, interests, job title, city, education, and prompt answers are stored as encrypted fields, which means they are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud regardless of your other iCloud settings.

To stop syncing, turn off Dating Profile Vault under Settings › your name › iCloud › Apps Using iCloud. Your data stays on the device.

Vault Keyboard

The optional Vault Keyboard pastes your saved bio and prompt answers into other apps. It reads a copy of your vault from a container that only this app and its keyboard can access. iOS requires "Allow Full Access" for any keyboard that reads its app's data; that is the only reason the keyboard asks for it. The keyboard has no network access, does not record what you type, and never sends anything anywhere.

AI Features

Dating Profile Vault uses Apple Intelligence for AI-powered rewrite suggestions. This processing:

  • Occurs entirely on your device
  • Is handled by Apple's FoundationModels framework
  • Never sends your content to external AI services
  • Requires compatible hardware (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iPad/Mac with Apple Silicon)

Analytics

The app uses TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage statistics—for example, that a bio was rewritten or a prompt was added. These signals are anonymized on your device before they are sent, are never linked to you, and never include the text of your profile. We use them only to understand which features are used. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The app is free and has no in-app purchases. Other than the anonymous analytics described above and Apple's own iCloud, it does not integrate with any advertising, purchase, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and your profile content is never transmitted to us or to any third party. When you use the copy feature to move content to dating apps, you manually copy text to your clipboard—no automatic transmission occurs.

Data Deletion

Delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing or editing your bio, interests, and prompts within the app
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all data on that device)
  • Deleting the app's iCloud data under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
