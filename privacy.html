Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Dating Profile Vault is designed with your privacy as a priority. Your dating profile content stays on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. All information you enter into the app—including bios, interests, job titles, locations, and prompt answers—is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics and purchase validation described below, neither of which includes your profile content.

Data Storage

  • All profile data is stored exclusively on your device
  • Data persists in your device's local storage
  • App settings are stored locally using UserDefaults
  • No cloud sync, no server uploads, no external databases

AI Features

Dating Profile Vault uses Apple Intelligence for AI-powered rewrite suggestions. This processing:

  • Occurs entirely on your device
  • Is handled by Apple's FoundationModels framework
  • Never sends your content to external AI services
  • Requires compatible hardware (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iPad/Mac with Apple Silicon)

Analytics

The app uses TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage statistics—for example, that a bio was rewritten or a prompt was added. These signals are anonymized on your device before they are sent, are never linked to you, and never include the text of your profile. We use them only to understand which features are used. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat, which receives purchase receipts and an anonymous, app-generated identifier to validate your subscription. No profile content is shared with RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Other than the anonymous analytics and purchase processing described above, this app does not integrate with any advertising or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and your profile content is never transmitted to us or to any third party. When you use the copy feature to move content to dating apps, you manually copy text to your clipboard—no automatic transmission occurs.

Data Deletion

Delete your data at any time by:

  • Removing individual profiles within the app
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all associated data)

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact
